Learning Outcomes

By the end of the programme, participants will be able to:

Explain the concepts of AI, Generative AI, Prompt Engineering, and Responsible AI.
Create professional business documents, reports, emails, presentations, and marketing content using AI.
Conduct AI-assisted market research, competitor analysis, and business intelligence.
Develop AI-powered social media campaigns, customer personas, and digital marketing assets.
Analyse business data and generate dashboards and insights using AI-enabled productivity tools.
Utilize AI for HR, finance, operations, customer support, and decision-making.
Automate repetitive business tasks using AI-powered workflow automation tools.
Design and present an AI-enabled business solution as a capstone project.
Demonstrate awareness of AI ethics, privacy, governance, and responsible business use.
