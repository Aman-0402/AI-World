Proposal: AI Inventory Management Assistant

1. Problem
- Warehouse team manually tracks stock across 3 locations
- Stock count mismatches happen almost every week
- Restocking decisions are often delayed or based on guesswork

2. Proposed Solution
- Implement an AI-powered inventory assistant that tracks stock levels
  in real time and predicts when items need reordering

3. Benefits
- Fewer stock-out incidents
- Reduced manual counting work
- Better demand forecasting
- Faster restocking decisions

4. Implementation
Phase 1: Connect inventory data sources
Phase 2: Configure AI tracking and alerts
Phase 3: Test with one warehouse location
Phase 4: Full rollout across all locations
Phase 5: Monitor and refine predictions

5. Estimated Cost
₹1,50,000 initial implementation cost

6. Timeline
Approximately 5 weeks

7. Recommendation
Proceed with a pilot at one warehouse location before expanding to all three
