JOB POSTING

Position: Marketing Intern
Company: BrightWave Consumer Goods
Location: Hybrid (2 days/week in office)
Duration: 3-month internship (possible extension)

ABOUT THE ROLE

We're looking for a Marketing Intern to support our brand and digital marketing team. You'll work closely with the Marketing Manager and Content team to help plan, create, and analyze campaigns across social media, email, and our website.

RESPONSIBILITIES

- Assist in creating content for Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok, including captions, graphics, and short-form video ideas
- Support email marketing campaigns using Mailchimp, including drafting copy and scheduling sends
- Track and report on social media engagement metrics (likes, shares, reach, click-through rate) using tools like Google Analytics and native platform insights
- Conduct competitor research and market trend analysis to inform campaign strategy
- Help coordinate influencer outreach and partnership logistics
- Assist with organizing and updating the content calendar
- Support the team in preparing marketing decks and campaign performance summaries using PowerPoint/Canva

REQUIREMENTS

- Currently pursuing a degree in Marketing, Business, Communications, or related field
- Strong written and verbal communication skills
- Familiarity with social media platforms (Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok) from a brand/business perspective
- Basic knowledge of Canva, Google Analytics, or Mailchimp is a plus
- Comfortable working with spreadsheets (Excel/Google Sheets) for tracking data
- Detail-oriented, organized, and able to manage multiple small tasks at once
- Interest in consumer branding and digital marketing trends

WHAT YOU'LL GAIN

- Hands-on experience with real marketing campaigns and analytics tools
- Exposure to content strategy, email marketing, and social media management
- Mentorship from the marketing team and cross-functional exposure to sales and design
- A letter of recommendation and portfolio-ready work samples upon successful completion

TO APPLY

Send your resume and a short note on why you're interested in marketing to careers@brightwaveconsumer.com.
