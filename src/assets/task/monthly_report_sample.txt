Monthly Project Report – Project Atlas – July 2026
Prepared by: Aman Raj, Project Lead

1. Overview
Project Atlas is on track for its Q3 milestones, with core development
progressing across backend, frontend, and QA workstreams. This report
covers progress, risks, and financials for the month of July.

2. Key Results
- Backend authentication module completed and passed QA testing
- Database migration to the new cloud infrastructure completed successfully,
  with zero downtime during the cutover
- Frontend dashboard redesign is 70% complete, slightly behind the original
  schedule due to a two-week delay in design handoff
- Automated deployment pipeline for staging environment is now fully
  operational, reducing manual deployment time from 2 hours to 15 minutes
- Client satisfaction survey (mid-project checkpoint) returned a score of
  8.2/10, up from 7.4/10 in the previous quarter

3. Risks
- Third-party payment gateway vendor has not delivered updated API
  documentation for six weeks, delaying payment integration work
- One senior QA engineer was on medical leave for two weeks in July,
  creating a testing backlog of approximately 40 test cases
- Load testing has not yet started; originally scheduled for early July,
  now at risk of slipping into August
- Team velocity dropped from an average of 40 story points per sprint to
  34 points in the last two sprints, attributed to the QA staffing gap
- Client requested two additional fields on the signup form mid-sprint,
  representing a minor scope change not accounted for in the original
  project budget

4. Financials
- Total spend for July: within 3% of planned monthly budget
- Overall project budget tracking 5% under plan year-to-date, primarily
  due to lower-than-expected infrastructure costs after the cloud migration
- No additional budget requests submitted this month

5. Team Updates
- Two new engineers onboarded to the frontend team, ramp-up expected to
  take 3-4 weeks before full productivity
- Design team completed all wireframes for Phase 2 of the project ahead
  of schedule
- Internal retrospective identified communication gaps between design and
  frontend teams as a contributing factor to the dashboard delay

6. Recommendations
- Escalate the payment gateway vendor delay through procurement, as the
  six-week gap now threatens the planned September launch date
- Temporarily reassign one backend engineer to support QA testing until
  the affected engineer returns, to prevent further backlog growth
- Formally log the signup form field change as a scope addition and route
  it through the standard change-request process before further work
  continues
- Begin load testing in the first week of August at the latest, even with
  partial feature completeness, to avoid compressing the testing window
  before launch
- Introduce a shared design-to-frontend handoff checklist to reduce future
  delays similar to the dashboard redesign slip

7. Next Steps
- Finance to review and respond to the vendor escalation by August 5
- QA lead to reassess testing timeline once backlog is reduced
- Project lead to schedule a scope-change discussion with the client by
  August 3
- Next monthly report due September 1
