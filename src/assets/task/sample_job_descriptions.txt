SAMPLE JOB DESCRIPTIONS — FOR PRACTICE
=========================================
Note: These are realistic practice postings for the activity. Students
who can access a job site are still encouraged to find a REAL posting
for a role they're personally interested in (Step 1). Use these if you
need a backup option or want extra practice on more than one posting.

---------------------------------------------------------------
JOB DESCRIPTION 1 — Marketing Executive
---------------------------------------------------------------
Company: Rovex Consumer Products
Location: Bengaluru, India (Hybrid)

About the Role:
We are looking for a Marketing Executive to support our brand and
digital marketing initiatives across product lines. The ideal
candidate will assist in campaign planning, content coordination,
and performance tracking.

Responsibilities:
- Assist in planning and executing digital marketing campaigns across
  social media, email, and paid ads
- Coordinate with design and content teams to prepare marketing
  collateral
- Track and report campaign performance using Google Analytics and
  Meta Business Suite
- Conduct competitor and market research to identify trends
- Support the marketing manager in budget tracking and vendor
  coordination
- Assist with organizing promotional events and product launches

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Marketing, or related
  field
- 0-2 years of experience (freshers welcome)
- Basic understanding of digital marketing tools (Google Ads, Meta
  Ads Manager, Canva)
- Strong written and verbal communication skills
- Good analytical and organizational skills
- Ability to multitask and work under deadlines
- Proficiency in MS Excel and PowerPoint

Preferred:
- Internship experience in marketing or a related field
- Familiarity with SEO basics and content marketing

JOB DESCRIPTION 2 — Human Resources Associate
---------------------------------------------------------------
Company: Nexbridge Technologies Pvt Ltd
Location: Pune, India (On-site)

About the Role:
Nexbridge is hiring an HR Associate to support end-to-end recruitment,
onboarding, and employee engagement activities for our growing team
of 200+ employees.

Responsibilities:
- Manage end-to-end recruitment for junior and mid-level positions,
  including sourcing, screening, and scheduling interviews
- Coordinate onboarding for new hires, including documentation and
  orientation sessions
- Maintain and update employee records in the HRMS system
- Assist in organizing employee engagement activities and internal
  events
- Support the HR manager with performance review cycle coordination
- Address basic employee queries related to policies and benefits
- Assist in preparing HR reports and attrition data summaries

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or
  related field
- 1-3 years of relevant HR experience
- Strong interpersonal and communication skills
- Working knowledge of MS Office and HRMS tools
- Ability to handle confidential information with discretion
- Good organizational and time-management skills

Preferred:
- Experience with applicant tracking systems (ATS)
- Basic knowledge of labor laws and HR compliance

JOB DESCRIPTION 3 — Sales Trainee
---------------------------------------------------------------
Company: Meridian FinServ
Location: Ahmedabad, India (Field + Office)

About the Role:
We are looking for energetic Sales Trainees to join our financial
products sales team. This role offers hands-on training in B2C sales,
client relationship management, and financial product knowledge.

Responsibilities:
- Generate leads through field visits, referrals, and cold calling
- Explain financial products (insurance, mutual funds, loans) to
  prospective clients
- Meet monthly sales targets assigned by the sales manager
- Build and maintain long-term relationships with clients
- Maintain accurate records of client interactions in the CRM
- Attend daily briefings and weekly sales training sessions
- Follow up with existing clients for renewals and cross-selling
  opportunities

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in any discipline (BBA/B.Com preferred)
- Freshers welcome; 0-1 years of experience
- Strong communication and persuasion skills
- Willingness to travel locally for client meetings
- Basic computer skills, familiarity with CRM tools is a plus
- Target-driven mindset with good negotiation skills

Preferred:
- Prior internship in sales or customer-facing roles
- Two-wheeler and valid driving license (for field visits)

JOB DESCRIPTION 4 — Operations Analyst
---------------------------------------------------------------
Company: Kestrel Logistics Pvt Ltd
Location: Mumbai, India (On-site)

About the Role:
Kestrel Logistics is seeking an Operations Analyst to support daily
operational planning, data analysis, and process improvement across
our warehouse and delivery network.

Responsibilities:
- Monitor daily operational metrics such as delivery timelines,
  order fulfillment rates, and warehouse throughput
- Prepare weekly and monthly operational reports for management
  review
- Identify bottlenecks in the supply chain and recommend
  process improvements
- Coordinate with warehouse, transport, and customer service teams
  to resolve operational issues
- Support the operations manager in vendor performance tracking
- Assist in preparing SOPs for new operational processes
- Use Excel and internal dashboards to analyze operational data

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Operations
  Management, Supply Chain, or related field
- 0-2 years of experience (freshers with strong analytical skills
  will be considered)
- Proficiency in MS Excel, including pivot tables and basic formulas
- Strong problem-solving and analytical thinking skills
- Good communication skills to coordinate across departments
- Attention to detail and comfort working with data

Preferred:
- Exposure to logistics, supply chain, or e-commerce operations
- Basic knowledge of Power BI or similar reporting tools

JOB DESCRIPTION 5 — Business Development Executive
---------------------------------------------------------------
Company: Zenlink Software Solutions
Location: Hyderabad, India (Hybrid)

About the Role:
We are hiring a Business Development Executive to identify new
business opportunities, build client relationships, and support
revenue growth for our B2B software products.

Responsibilities:
- Identify and research potential clients through market research
  and networking
- Reach out to prospective clients via email, LinkedIn, and calls to
  generate qualified leads
- Schedule and support product demo calls with the sales team
- Maintain accurate lead and pipeline data in the CRM
- Prepare proposals and follow up on business inquiries
- Collaborate with the marketing team on lead generation campaigns
- Track and report weekly progress against business development
  targets

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Business Administration, Marketing, or
  related field
- 0-2 years of experience in sales, business development, or
  related roles
- Excellent verbal and written communication skills
- Confident and proactive approach to client outreach
- Basic understanding of B2B sales processes
- Comfortable using CRM tools, LinkedIn, and email outreach platforms

Preferred:
- Prior internship experience in business development or sales
- Interest in the software/technology industry
